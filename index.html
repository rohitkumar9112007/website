About the Campus Food-Tech System

Campus Problem: During the 12:30–2:00 PM peak interval, food courts at Uni-Mall and academic blocks experience bottleneck queues of 25–40 students per counter. Students spend most of their lunch window waiting, leading to skipped meals or late arrival to consecutive classes.

The Engineering Solution: An integrated pre-order workflow synchronised with lecture timetables. Students queue up virtually, meal preparation is staggered to lecture release times, and pickup is facilitated via single-use encrypted QR tokens at dedicated express windows.
